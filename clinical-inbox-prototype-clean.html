Clinical Inbox Prioritization

A mobile inbox for doctors that suggests which items to open first, and explains why. The doctor always decides.

The problem

Doctors check their phone inbox between patients. Lab results, patient messages, refills, and billing notes all arrive in one list, newest first. A critical result can sit under a reschedule request, and nothing says which to open first.

The solution

The inbox suggests an order. Urgent items move to the top with a plain reason and a confidence level. The doctor can overrule it, give feedback, or go back to newest first. The app never replies, closes, or hides anything.

How we know it works

Urgent items are seen sooner, with no rise in missed ones. The measure is time to first urgent review. Missed urgent items is the release gate. An admin screen shows the team how it is doing and can switch it off.

How to use this demo

Switch between Before and After to compare. Try the scenarios under the toggle: a locked-phone alert, suggestions failing, and no signal. Open the Admin view to see the team's side.
